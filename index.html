Scale “this use case” in your own environment

This is where play turns into production. A retailer who likes a sandbox demo moves to a scoped build in their own AWS account:

  • Discovery — AWS maps the use case to your data & systems
  • Pilot — a scoped proof-of-value on your real data
  • Partner match — the right ISV from the AWS ecosystem joins the build
  • Scale — deployed, owned, and running in your environment

In the live Playground, this would capture the retailer's interest and route it to the AWS × SRA team as a qualified lead.
